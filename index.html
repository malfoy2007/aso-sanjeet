About Me

A little about the person behind the journey.

SK

Hello, I'm Sanjeet.

I was born on 10 December 2006 in a small farmer family in Bihar.

My hometown is Bijubigha, a small village about 10 km from Pakribarawan in Nawada district.

Coming from a simple background, education became an important part of my life. My journey has not always been straight or perfect. There have been interruptions, mistakes, failures, hard work and moments of happiness.

This website is my attempt to preserve those moments — not just the achievements, but the journey behind them.

“Life is short. Live today. Spend your life the way you feel you should, without constantly worrying about what people say.”
— My Life Philosophy

My Journey

The important chapters of my school life.

Childhood

Bijubigha, Nawada

I grew up in Bijubigha, a small village in Bihar, in a farmer family. My early life was simple and closely connected with village life.

School Years

Divya Gyan Niketan

I studied up to Class 7 at Divya Gyan Niketan, a school run by my uncle, Dinesh Prasad.

COVID-19 Period

An Unusual Break

The COVID-19 pandemic disrupted my schooling. I did not study Class 8 in the usual way and later studied Class 9 twice before moving forward toward matriculation.

Matriculation

D.P. Roy +2 High School

I joined D.P. Roy +2 High School for my matriculation. Looking back, this became one of the most memorable chapters of my school life.

Mathematics Olympiad

District Level Achievement

My mathematics teacher, Sanjay Sir, encouraged me and selected me for the district-level Mathematics Olympiad. I gave my best and secured the highest marks at the district level.

A Memorable Day

My Name in the Newspaper

I received a prize of ₹20,000 from the District Magistrate. For the first time, I saw my name prominently printed in a Hindi newspaper. That was an unforgettable moment for me.

Class 10

A Late Realisation

During the beginning of Class 10, I did not study as seriously as I should have. Later I realised that if I wanted success, I had to change my approach.

The Final Push

1.5 Months of Hard Work

In the final stage of Class 10, I left coaching and focused on studying at home. I worked hard for about one and a half months.

Matriculation Result

457 / 500

I scored 457 out of 500 and topped my school. I was not as happy as I had imagined I would be, but seeing my parents and brother happy made the day meaningful.
